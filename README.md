EMBERFALL DUEL is an original tactical duel card battler: Asha, the Ember Knight, faces Vex, the Frost Warden, across a table. Both duelists have 30 life and ember that grows by 1 each turn (max 10). Four suits — Ember, Tide, Thorn and Gale — are told apart by colour and shape. Play creatures onto the table, cast spells, and attack with your creatures to reduce Vex to 0 life. The AI plays creatures, casts spells and attacks with readable priorities (lethal first, then good trades, then face), one action at a time so you can follow it. Pure vanilla JavaScript and Canvas 2D — no libraries, no network, no build step. Controls: mouse to play cards and attack, Space to end your turn, Esc back to the title screen, M to mute the procedural WebAudio sounds.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.
